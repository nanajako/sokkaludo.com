Rules of Soccer Ludo

⚽ Match Objective: Navigate your team's players around the stadium pitch and down your team's goal corridor to score goals in the central Goal Box!

1. Pitch Entry: Roll a 6 to bring a player off the bench and onto your starting tile.

2. Tackling & Interceptions: Landing on an opponent's tile tackles them, takes possession, and sends their player back to their bench (Over / Out)!

3. Corner Kicks (Yellow Tiles): Landing here launches your player 3 extra steps forward with a long cross.

4. Throw-Ins (Orange Tiles): Retain possession with an immediate bonus dice roll.

5. Scoring Goals: Enter the central Goal Box with an exact roll. First team to 2 Goals wins the championship!
